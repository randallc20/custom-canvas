# Shipping, Returns & Refunds Policy

Custom Canvas LLC · support@customcanvas.shop · Version 1.0 · Effective: September 3, 2026

This policy is part of the Terms of Sale. Refunds come first; shipping follows.

---

## Refunds — the short version

**There is no automatic right of return** if you simply change your mind. That decision
belongs to the artist. If the artist approves, Custom Canvas settles the money.

**Four things are not the artist's decision**, and on these we refund you whether or not
they agree:

1. A piece that **never arrives**
2. A piece that arrives **damaged**
3. A piece that is **materially not as described**
4. An order that is **never shipped**

On a change-of-mind refund you receive **the artwork price + shipping + the sales tax
charged on those amounts**; the **service fee, and the tax charged on that fee, are not
refunded**. Where the refund is for one of the four reasons above, we refund the service
fee as well.

The artist is the seller of your piece, but Custom Canvas administers refunds. If you
cannot get a fair answer from an artist, write to us.

## Why it works this way

Every piece here is a one-of-a-kind object made by an individual person and shipped from
their studio, not stock pulled from a warehouse. A returned painting is a painting that
was crated, posted, handled and returned, and the artist carries that cost personally.
So the change-of-mind decision is theirs.

## How to request a refund

1. Open the order in **Orders** and choose **Request a refund**. This opens a message
   thread with the artist, pre-filled with your order number.
2. Explain what is wrong. Photographs help, especially for damage.
3. The artist responds. If they approve, Custom Canvas settles it, and you will see the
   order move to refunded.
4. Refunds return to your original payment method. Your bank typically posts it within
   5–10 business days.

## Returning the artwork

Do not return artwork without return instructions from Custom Canvas or the artist
through the Platform.

An authorized return must ordinarily be shipped within seven calendar days, using the
supplied address, reasonable protective packaging, tracking, and any insurance
specified in the instructions.

For an approved change-of-mind return, you ordinarily pay return shipping and bear risk
of loss until delivery. For damaged, materially misdescribed, or incorrectly supplied
artwork, the artist ordinarily pays reasonable return-shipping costs and bears
return-transit risk, provided you follow the instructions.

A refund may be issued after the artwork is returned and reasonably inspected. You may
not retain the artwork and receive a full refund unless Custom Canvas determines that
returning it would be unlawful, unsafe, disproportionately expensive, or otherwise
inappropriate.

## Damaged or not as described

Different from changing your mind, and treated differently.

If a piece arrives **damaged**, or is **materially not what the listing described** —
wrong medium, wrong size, a reproduction sold as an original, undisclosed damage —
contact the artist and then **support@customcanvas.shop**. Keep the packaging and
photograph everything, including the box.

**Where we substantiate that a piece arrived damaged or was materially misdescribed,
Custom Canvas will refund you whether or not the artist agrees.**

**Report promptly.** Report visible shipping damage within 48 hours after delivery and
other material problems within seven calendar days after delivery. A report should
include photographs of the artwork, packaging, shipping label, and the damage or
inconsistency.

Failure to report within these periods may materially impair our ability to recover
from a carrier or investigate the claim, but it does not eliminate any nonwaivable legal
right or prevent Custom Canvas from considering a later claim based on fraud, concealed
damage, or circumstances that reasonably prevented earlier notice.

## If your piece is never shipped

Artists ship within **5 business days**. If that passes, the artist should offer you a
new date or a cancellation, and **you do not have to accept the new date**. Choose
cancellation and you get a full refund. If the artist stops responding and neither you
nor we can reach them within five business days, we cancel the order and refund you
without their approval.

## Cancellations

Ask the artist to cancel before they ship and they usually can. Once a piece is posted,
it is a refund question rather than a cancellation.

## Before you dispute with your bank

Please talk to us first. A chargeback takes weeks and we can usually resolve things in
days. If you do dispute, we respond with the records we hold, including tracking and
delivery confirmation.

## Commissions

Commissioned work is arranged directly between you and the artist and is **not** covered
by this policy. Currently, commissions are paid off-platform.

Nothing here limits rights you have under law that cannot be excluded.

# Shipping

## Who ships

The **artist** packs and ships your piece directly from their studio. Custom Canvas
never handles the artwork.

## Timing

Artists ship within **5 business days** of purchase. That window is shown on every
listing before you buy. You will receive tracking when the piece is posted.

Business days are Monday to Friday.

**If the window is missed.** The artist should tell you in Messages before it expires
and offer you either a new date or a cancellation. You are free to refuse the new date.
If you do, you may cancel for a **full refund**, and it is not the artist's decision to
make.

## Cost

Shipping is set **per piece by the artist** and shown before you pay. Local pickup is
free.

## Carriers and tracking

Artists ship via **USPS, UPS, FedEx or DHL**, with tracking recorded on the order.
Artists are required to obtain **signature confirmation** on orders of **$750 or more**.

## Where we ship

**United States only** currently. International shipping is not offered.

## Your address

The delivery address you enter at checkout is the one used — and it determines the sales
tax charged. Please check it before paying. Once a piece has been posted it generally
cannot be rerouted.

## Local pickup

Some artists offer pickup instead of shipping. You will not be charged shipping. You and
the artist must arrange pickup through Custom Canvas Messages, and unless otherwise
agreed, pickup should occur within seven calendar days after the order is ready.

Inspect the artwork before accepting possession. Both of you should record completion
using the Platform's pickup-confirmation process or, until that feature is available, a
clear written confirmation in Messages. That written confirmation is what protects both
of you if there is later a dispute about whether the piece changed hands.

If you fail to appear or do not arrange pickup after reasonable reminders, the artist
must contact Custom Canvas before canceling the order, charging any storage amount, or
treating the artwork as abandoned. No storage fee may be imposed unless it was disclosed
and affirmatively accepted before the fee began accruing.

## Damage in transit

Photograph the packaging and the piece before unpacking further, keep everything, and
contact the artist and support@customcanvas.shop promptly.

## Risk of loss

**The artist bears the risk until your piece is delivered.** If it is lost, stolen or
damaged in transit you are entitled to a refund of the artwork price and shipping, and
that refund is not at the artist's discretion.

For shipped orders, risk of loss remains with the artist until carrier records show
delivery to the address stated in the Custom Canvas order. For orders requiring
signature confirmation, risk does not transfer until the required signature or other
approved delivery confirmation is obtained.

After valid delivery, risk ordinarily transfers to you, including risk of theft or
damage occurring after delivery. Custom Canvas may nevertheless investigate evidence of
misdelivery, fraudulent delivery confirmation, or delivery to an incorrect address.

For local pickup, risk transfers when you or your authorized recipient take possession
and confirm the handoff.

## Shipping coverage

Artists must purchase commercially reasonable shipping coverage for the artwork price
when such coverage is reasonably available. If full coverage is unavailable because of
carrier exclusions, value limits, artwork type, or destination, the artist must disclose
that issue to Custom Canvas before shipping and follow any alternative shipping
instructions.

Your contractual right to an approved refund for nondelivery or transit damage does not
depend on whether the artist successfully recovers from a carrier or insurer.

Shipping coverage is maintained for the artist's benefit and does not make Custom Canvas
an insurer, carrier, or guarantor of the shipment.
