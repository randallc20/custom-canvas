# DMCA & Copyright Policy

Custom Canvas LLC · support@customcanvas.shop · Version 1.0 · Effective: September 3, 2026

---

## Our position

Custom Canvas respects copyright. Artists warrant that they own or control the rights to
everything they list. We remove material we are properly notified is infringing, and we
terminate repeat infringers.

## Reporting infringement

Send a written notice to our designated agent containing all of the following. An
incomplete notice may not trigger our obligations.

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work you say is infringed.
3. Identification of the material on Custom Canvas you say infringes, with enough
   detail for us to find it — **a direct URL to the listing or profile**.
4. Your name, address, telephone number and email.
5. A statement that you believe **in good faith** that the use is not authorized by the
   copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and **under penalty of
   perjury**, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

## What we do on receiving a notice

When Custom Canvas receives a notice that substantially satisfies the DMCA's
requirements, we may promptly remove or disable access to the identified material. We
will take reasonable steps to notify the affected user and may provide that user with a
copy or summary of the notice, including the claimant's contact information.

Custom Canvas may request clarification when a notice is incomplete, does not identify
the material with reasonable specificity, or does not appear to concern copyright
infringement.

Removal of material is not a determination that infringement occurred, and restoration
of material is not a determination that the material is lawful.

## Counter-notice

If your work was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, send a
counter-notice containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the removed material and where it appeared.
3. A statement **under penalty of perjury** that you believe in good faith it was
   removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address and telephone number, and a statement consenting to the
   jurisdiction of the federal court for your district (or, if outside the US, any
   district where we may be found), and that you will accept service from the person who
   gave the original notice.

By submitting a counter-notice, you acknowledge that it may be forwarded to the person
who submitted the original notice.

If Custom Canvas receives a substantially compliant counter-notice, we will forward it
to the original claimant. Custom Canvas may restore the material not less than 10 and
not more than 14 business days after receiving the counter-notice unless the designated
agent first receives notice that the claimant filed a qualifying court action seeking to
restrain the alleged infringement.

Custom Canvas may decline restoration when the material independently violates the
Terms of Service, Listing Standards, or applicable law.

## Repeat infringers

Custom Canvas terminates, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who
repeatedly infringe the copyrights of others.

We assess repeat infringement on the total circumstances rather than a fixed count.
Relevant factors include the number of substantially compliant notices concerning a
user, whether a counter-notice was filed and accepted, whether a notice was withdrawn or
plainly defective, the volume and nature of the material, whether the user was
previously warned, whether the conduct appears deliberate or commercial in scale, and
whether the user cooperated in removing the material.

As general guidance, **three or more substantiated notices within twelve months** will
ordinarily result in termination. A notice is ordinarily not counted where the user
files a counter-notice that we accept, where the notice is withdrawn, or where the
notice is plainly defective.

Custom Canvas may terminate an account immediately, without regard to the number of
prior notices, for deliberate or large-scale infringement, such as wholesale copying of
another artist's catalog, or where termination is required by law.

Custom Canvas may also take action short of termination, including removing material,
restricting listing privileges, issuing a warning, or suspending an account while a
matter is reviewed.

Nothing in this section limits Custom Canvas's right to remove content or close an
account for any other violation of the Terms of Service, the Artist Agreement, or the
Listing Standards.

## Not a substitute for the Listing Standards

A DMCA notice addresses **copyright**: who owns an image. Claims that a work is
**forged, misattributed or misdescribed** address whether a piece is what it claims to
be, and are handled under the **Listing Standards**. Different problem, different
process.
