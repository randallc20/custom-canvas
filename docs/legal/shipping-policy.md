# Shipping Policy

**DRAFT — for counsel review.**

---

## Who ships

The **artist** packs and ships your piece directly from their studio. Custom Canvas
never handles the artwork.

## Timing

Artists ship within **5 business days** of purchase. That window is shown on every
listing before you buy. You will receive tracking when the piece is posted.

Business days are Monday to Friday. If an artist cannot meet the window, they should
tell you in Messages and offer to cancel.

## Cost

Shipping is set **per piece by the artist** and shown before you pay. Local pickup is
free.

## Carriers and tracking

Artists ship via **USPS, UPS, FedEx or DHL**, with tracking recorded on the order.
Orders of **$750 or more** ship with **signature confirmation**.

## Where we ship

**United States only** at launch.

## Your address

The delivery address you enter at checkout is the one used — and it determines the sales
tax charged. Please check it before paying. Once a piece has been posted it generally
cannot be rerouted.

## Local pickup

Some artists offer pickup instead of shipping. You will not be charged shipping, and
after paying you arrange the handoff with the artist in Messages.

**Confirm the handoff in the Custom Canvas message thread.** That written confirmation
is what protects both of you if there is later a dispute about whether the piece
changed hands.

## Damage in transit

Photograph the packaging and the piece before unpacking further, keep everything, and
contact the artist and support@customcanvas.shop promptly.

[**RISK OF LOSS — COUNSEL TO SET.** When title and risk pass to the buyer, who is
required to insure a shipment, and who bears a carrier loss, are all currently
undefined across every document. This is the most consequential open gap in the buyer
documents, and the answer must be consistent here, in the Terms of Sale, and in the
Artist Agreement.]

## International

Not offered at launch.
