# Community Guidelines

**DRAFT — for counsel review.**

Custom Canvas is a small marketplace built on local artists and the people who collect
from them. These are the ground rules.

---

## Be straight about your work

Describe pieces accurately — medium, size, condition, and whether something is an
original, an edition or a print. See the Authenticity & Provenance Policy.

## Be civil

No harassment, threats, slurs, or abuse — in messages, reviews, listings or profiles.
Disagreements about a sale are normal; contempt is not.

## Messages

Messages are for talking about art, commissions and orders. Do not use them for
unsolicited marketing, chain messages, or to push a buyer off-platform to avoid
commission on a relationship formed here.

You can **block** anyone. Blocking stops them contacting you.

## Reviews

Only a buyer with a delivered order can review it, once. Reviews should be honest,
first-hand, and about the work and the transaction.

Do not: write or solicit fake reviews, offer anything in exchange for a review, or
review your own work or a competitor's.

We remove reviews that are fake, incentivised, defamatory, or contain someone's personal
information. **We do not remove a review simply because it is negative.**

## Images

Post images of your own work. Do not post other artists' images, stock photography
presented as your work, or anything sexual involving minors — the last of which means
immediate closure and a report to the authorities.

## Reporting

Report a message or a listing from the item itself, or write to
**support@customcanvas.shop**. Tell us what you saw and where.

## What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity: we remove content, warn, suspend a shop, or close an account.
Where practical we explain why and give you a chance to respond. Illegal activity is
reported.

*Questions: support@customcanvas.shop*
